Addressing Your Doubts

Everything you need to know before your first practice

Sound Therapy

Is this a religious or esoteric practice?

No. Our approach is based on vibroacoustics and the laws of physics. Sound waves and the physical vibration of the instruments directly affect the nervous system, fascia, and muscles, helping the body release accumulated stress. This is profound work with your attention and relaxation, requiring no special beliefs or prior preparation.

Do I need to undress, and will there be physical contact?

The practice takes place in comfortable, loose clothing (we recommend soft fabrics without zippers). The practitioner interacts with you with absolute respect for your personal boundaries. The forged bowls are placed directly on the body over your clothes. If any touch is uncomfortable for you, we can conduct the session entirely through a non-contact sound field — which is also highly effective.

I am shy about showing emotions around others. Which format should I choose?

For maximum immersion and emotional release, we highly recommend an individual session (or a couples session for you and a loved one). We create a completely safe "cocoon" where you can fall asleep, cry, or exhale your tension without any outside observers. Group formats are excellent for lighter relaxation and meditation.

What if I don't know how to meditate and am afraid of looking foolish compared to others?

In sound therapy, there is no such thing as "right" or "wrong" meditation. You do not need to assume complex lotus postures or try to meet anyone's expectations. You simply lie down on a soft mat, cover yourself with a blanket, and close your eyes. Your body will do everything itself under the influence of vibration. We do not judge your experience — everyone lives through this practice individually, and any process you go through is absolutely normal and correct.

Can I interrupt the practice if I suddenly feel uncomfortable?

Yes, absolutely. You are in full control of the process. Our space is a zone of total safety and freedom. If at any moment during the session you feel the need to open your eyes, change your posture, drink water, or even quietly leave the room, you can do so calmly and without the slightest awkwardness. Your comfort is always our top priority.

Will the session help me if I have too many thoughts and cannot relax?

This is the most common doubt, and it is exactly what sound handles best. You do not need to purposefully "force" yourself not to think — this will only create more tension. The physical vibration of the instruments affects the body directly, bypassing the logical control of the mind. Even if your thoughts are chaotic at first, your nervous system will naturally switch into a state of deep relaxation after a short while.

Are there any medical contraindications for sound therapy?

Vibroacoustic massage is a gentle practice, but it has strict limitations. We do not conduct contact sessions (placing bowls on the body) if you have a pacemaker, are in the early stages of pregnancy, have epilepsy, severe psychiatric disorders, or acute inflammatory processes. Before starting our work, we always discuss your well-being to eliminate any risks and make the practice 100% safe for you.

What is the difference between a live session and a video with bowl sounds?

Audio in headphones only transmits the sound pattern, which is heavily compressed by electronics. In a live session, you receive a volumetric acoustic resonance and powerful physical vibration that literally travels through your body's tissues, relaxing spasms. Such tactile immersion cannot be simulated through phone speakers.

Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)

Is Jyotish about fortune-telling and predicting the future?

No, we do not engage in fatalism or fortune-telling. Vedic astrology Jyotish in our practice is a precise analytical tool, a kind of "map" of your psyche and karmic tasks. It helps you understand your strengths, the causes of current tension, and select methods to restore your resources, relying on your true nature rather than abstract predictions. But the ultimate Path is created by you — through the power of intention and the will of everyday choices.

Will the reading results be too general, "fitting just anyone"?

Jyotish is not abstract fortune-telling or popular magazine zodiac signs. It is an ancient Vedic scientific system — Vedanga ("Eyes of the Vedas"), based on precise astronomical and mathematical calculations of the starry sky at the exact moment of your birth.

Unlike simplified approaches, Vedic astrology works with your unique natal chart. We analyze not only the position of the planets (Grahas) but also the individual ascendant sign (Lagna), the Moon's nakshatra, as well as planetary combinations and subtle divisional charts. Every soul incarnates at a strictly specific time when the picture of the starry sky accurately reflects its accumulated karma. You will not receive "universal formulas," but a profound decoding of your personal psychological profile, hidden talents, decision-making algorithms, and karmic tasks.

Why go to a master if there are many free astrological calculators online?

Any online calculator only produces a dry, isolated software algorithm. It shows fragments — individual planetary positions that, without understanding the context, often contradict each other. A program lacks consciousness and cannot see the holistic picture of your destiny.

The main task of a master is synthesis. Jyotish carries profound philosophical meaning. The master connects hundreds of factors (Vimshottari-dasha periods, transits, house strengths, the Ashtakavarga system) into a single logical system, translating the wisdom of sacred shastras into an understandable modern language and adapting it to your real life. In a consultation, you are not paying for an automatic chart calculation, but for a living transmission of knowledge, years of practical experience, and a deep, applicable decoding of your path.

If everything is "written" in the natal chart, does that mean my destiny is predetermined and I cannot change anything?

No, fatalism contradicts the essence of the Vedas. A horoscope is a map of the soul, not the route itself. The natal chart shows your potential, accumulated karma (dharma), and main life lessons. However, you create the final Path yourself — through the power of your intention and daily conscious choices.

Planets do not "punish" or force us to act in a certain way. They work like a mirror, reflecting our own destiny in the material world. As stated in the Vedic scriptures, the goal of Jyotish is not to change the external circumstances themselves, but to change our reaction to them. Choosing whether to suffer from trials or grow spiritually through them is our personal choice. By gaining power over the mind and understanding the lessons of the planets, a person stops blindly obeying "fate" and achieves true freedom.

What if the astrologer sees something terrible in my chart? How dangerous is it to know?

In the ancient tradition, Jyotish was created not to frighten, but to provide protection, vision, and awareness. In the Vedic approach, there are no "death sentences". All tense combinations (such as a difficult Saturn period or Mars aspects) simply indicate areas where the soul needs to apply more attention, patience, and self-work.

If the chart indicates a period of weakness or potential challenges, Jyotish provides specific tools for neutralization and correction (Upayas). We do not use "scare tactics". You will receive practical Vedic methods of harmonization: breathwork (Svara-shastra), mantra therapy, harmonization through elements of Ayurveda and sound therapy, charity, and a proper spiritual mindset. Knowledge brings not fear, but support and the ability to step out from under the negative influence of planets.

Ayurveda

Do I need to understand doshas and Ayurveda to come for a consultation?

Absolutely not. Our task is to translate complex ancient terms into accessible modern language. You do not need to know what Pitta, Vata, or Kapha is, nor read specialized literature. We will conduct the diagnostics ourselves, determining your natural constitution and current imbalances. You will not get a lecture in Sanskrit, but a ready-made, simple "manual" for your body with practical, real-life recommendations.

Will Ayurveda definitely help me?

Ayurveda is not a "magic pill" that instantly erases symptoms, but a profound system of restoring health proven over millennia. It works flawlessly because it eliminates the very root cause of the imbalance in the body, rather than masking its consequences. The effect of the Ayurvedic approach is always noticeable and long-lasting, but it directly depends on your willingness to implement our recommendations into your life: gently adjusting your diet, daily routine, and habits according to your natural constitution.

What if I feel worse after a session (e.g., headache, anxiety, or severe fatigue)?

This condition does not occur often, but it is absolutely normal. Specialists call it a "healing crisis" or a stage of deep adaptation.

In sound therapy, the vibration of the bowls brings long-standing muscular and emotional blocks to the surface. If there was too much suppressed tension, its release might be accompanied by temporary weakness, a mild headache, or sudden tears. This does not mean you have been harmed — it means your nervous system has begun a massive "cleanup" and stress release.

In Ayurveda, the approach is structured to act as gently and safely as possible. The Vedic approach in the modern world is always integrated with common sense. We consider your existing medical diagnoses, physiological limitations, current test results, the stage of diseases, and medications prescribed by a doctor. But when we trigger cleansing processes and adjust habits, the body begins to actively get rid of accumulated toxins, which in the first few days can cause a slight feeling of fatigue.

The main rule: do not be frightened by this state. Drink plenty of warm water, give yourself time to rest, and soon this temporary discomfort will be replaced by a state of crystal clarity and profound lightness.
